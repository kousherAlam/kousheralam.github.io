Theatre

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Theatre is an Android app for Theatre, a self-hosted media server. The app shows and plays what is on a server you run and choose. It has no server of its own.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected by the developer. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no account with us.

What the app sends, and where

Everything the app sends goes only to the media server address you enter:

  • the username and password you sign in with (or a single sign-on session from the identity provider your server uses);
  • what you watch and where you stopped, how long you watched, the notes you write, and your star ratings;
  • for administrators: downloads you add, titles you rename or delete, and other changes you make on that server.

That server, and how it stores this, is run by you (or whoever gave you an account on it). Many such servers sit on a home network and use plain HTTP; use them only on networks you trust.

What stays on your phone

  • the list of server addresses you added and their names;
  • a sign-in session for each server, kept in Android's encrypted storage;
  • small preferences, such as your last subtitle language, whether autoplay is on and whether the notes panel was open;
  • cached pictures, to load faster.

Removing a server under Advance → Servers signs out of it and deletes its session. Uninstalling the app deletes all of the above.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to reach your server.
  • Wake lock: keeps the screen on while a video plays.

Files you choose (such as a .torrent file) are picked with Android's own file picker; the app only receives the file you pick.

Links you open

Links to other websites open in your browser, under that website's own privacy policy.

Children

Theatre is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the developer contact address shown on Theatre's Google Play listing.
